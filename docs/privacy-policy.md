# EdgeChat Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 16 September 2026_

EdgeChat is an offline AI chat app for iPhone and Android. It runs language models entirely on your device.

## What EdgeChat collects

**Nothing.** EdgeChat has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service and no server of its own.

- **Your conversations, photos and documents** never leave your device. They are stored in the app's private storage and are deleted when you delete a chat or uninstall the app.
- **Model inference** (the AI replies) runs on your phone's processor. No text, image or document is sent anywhere to generate a reply. Airplane mode works.
- **Network access** is used for exactly one thing: downloading model files you choose from the Models screen. Those downloads go directly to the model hosts (currently Hugging Face). Hugging Face's own privacy policy applies to that download request, which contains no data from your chats.
- **Diagnostics log.** The app keeps a small local log of engine warnings and per-reply statistics (token counts and speeds, never message text). It is only shared if you explicitly tap "Share diagnostics log" in Settings and choose where to send it.

## Permissions

- **Internet**: model downloads only.
- **Notifications** (Android): progress of model downloads.
- **Photos / files**: only the items you pick to attach to a message, read once and copied into the chat's private folder.

## Children

EdgeChat is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
